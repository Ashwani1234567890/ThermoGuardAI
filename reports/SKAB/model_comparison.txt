--------------------------------------
FINAL OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------------

1. All three models were successfully trained.
2. None is suitable for deployment in its
   current configuration.
3. Accuracy alone is insufficient for anomaly
   detection evaluation.
4. False negatives and false positives must
   both be considered.
5. SKAB sensor features differ from our
   intended 16-channel NTC thermal sensor data.
6. These experiments provide baseline
   benchmarking, not direct transfer of
   trained model weights to the thermal system.

NEXT PHASE:
Develop thermal-specific datasets and models
for static and temporal thermal threat detection.
